## 1. Introduction

This document defines the APTITUDE Trust Evaluation Profile for interoperable trust evaluation across the APTITUDE pilots.

It profiles the trust evaluation processes defined by the [APTITUDE Trust Specifications](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/). The profile separates common trust operations from checks that are specific to WRPAC authentication, WRPRC authorization, Register responses, and signatures or seals on attestations.

The detailed source processes are the Authentication Process, X.509 Certificate Chain Validation, and Sign/Seal Validation Process in the [Trust Evaluation Process](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/) section. These are:

- [Trust Anchor Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#trust-anchor-validation-process);
- [Authentication Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#authentication-process);
- [Authorization Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#authorization-process)
- [Signature Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/)

The purpose of this RFC is to define:

- the common validation of Trust Artifacts:
  
  - the WRPAC validation used to authenticate a WRP;
  - the mandatory WRPRC and optional Register-response checks used to establish authorization context;
  - the Sign/Seal Certificate and attestation signature validation checks;

- interfaces and conformance checks that can be converted into ITB+ test cases.

This RFC is a cross-pilot baseline. A pilot MAY add stricter requirements, but it SHALL NOT weaken the mandatory checks defined here.

## 2. Scope

This RFC covers the following trust evaluation layers:

1. **Trust-source validation:** validation of the applicable List of Trusted Entities (LoTE), followed by extraction of a Trust Anchor from `ServiceDigitalIdentity`.
2. **Common X.509 validation:** validation of a certificate path against the extracted Trust Anchor.
3. **WRPAC validation:** authentication of a WRP using its WRPAC and a signature over the interaction artifact.
4. **WRPRC validation:** validation of the WRP registration certificate as authorization evidence.
5. **Register-response validation:** optional validation of the applicable signed or sealed Registrar response in addition to the WRPRC.
6. **Sign/Seal validation:** validation of the certificate used to sign or seal an Attestation and verification of the Attestation signature or seal.

APTITUDE profiles use LoTEs only. They do not use a List of Trusted Lists (LOTL), a national Trusted List (TL), or a LOTL-to-TL discovery hierarchy. A LoTE MAY be published in JSON or XML, as specified for its entity type.

Each entity type SHALL have a dedicated LoTE. WP2 SHALL manage and publish the LoTEs used by that pilot, including the relevant publication endpoints and applicable profiles. The applicable LoTE is selected by the artifact or entity type being validated.

The QTSPs Trust Anchors SHALL be published in a dedicated QEAA-provider LoTE. The pilot is expected to publish this LoTE in XML; the final XML publication and signature profile remain a WP2 configuration point.

This RFC includes baseline requirements for:

- Wallet Units;
- Wallet Relying Parties;
- Providers of WRPAC and WRPRC;
- Attestation and PID Providers;
- LoTE management and publication by the WP2 lead organization;
- Registrars and Registers.

This RFC does not define:

- sector-specific certificate semantics, which are defined by the relevant WRPAC, WRPRC, and Sign/Seal Certificate [profiles](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-artifacts/);
- the administrative [lifecycle](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-management-lifecycle/) of entities and Trust Anchors;
- revocation mechanisms or revocation endpoint protocols, which are outside this document's detailed scope and SHALL be referenced through RFC004;
- pilot-specific user-interface requirements;
- detailed implementation assertions beyond the conformance catalogue in this document.

## 4. Roles and Components

### 4.1 Wallet Unit

The Wallet Unit, including its, performs the trust evaluation on behalf of the User.

### 4.2 Wallet Relying Party

The Wallet Relying Party (WRP) is the entity authenticated with a WRPAC. Depending on the interaction, it may be a direct Relying Party or a Relying Party Intermediary.

### 4.3 Provider of WRPAC

The Provider of WRPAC issues WRPACs. Its Trust Anchor certificates SHALL be published in the dedicated WRPAC-provider LoTE.

### 4.4 Provider of WRPRC

The Provider of WRPRC issues WRPRCs. Its Trust Anchor certificates SHALL be published in the dedicated WRPRC-provider LoTE.

### 4.5 Attestation Provider and QTSP

An Attestation Provider issues an Attestation and signs or seals it using a Sign/Seal Certificate. The Trust Anchor certificates for the applicable Sign/Seal Certificate issuers SHALL be published in the LoTE dedicated to the relevant entity type, including the QEAA-provider LoTE for QEAA providers.

### 4.6 LoTE Publisher

The LoTE Publisher is the WP2 trust group within the pilot. It SHALL manage and publish one LoTE per configured entity type and SHALL make the applicable format and endpoint available to relying implementations.

### 4.7 Registrar

The Registrar and its APIs are managed and published the WP2 trust group within the pilot. This service stores WRP registration data and exposes the Register API. The Registrar's Trust Anchor certificates SHALL be published in the dedicated Registrar and Register LoTE.

## 5. Trust Model and High-Level Flows

### 5.1 Entity-Type LoTE Model

The following table defines the minimum LoTE separation for this profile.

| Entity type | Trust source | Expected format | Used to validate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wallet Providers | Wallet Provider LoTE | JSON | WUAs, WIAs, and related wallet artifacts |
| PID Providers | PID Provider LoTE | JSON | PID signatures |
| PuB-EAA Providers | PuB-EAA Provider LoTE | JSON | PuB-EAA signatures |
| Providers of WRPAC | WRPAC-provider LoTE | JSON | WRPAC chains |
| Providers of WRPRC | WRPRC-provider LoTE | JSON | WRPRC signatures and chains |
| Registrars and Registers | Registrar and Register LoTE | JSON | Register responses |
| QEAA providers and QTSPs | QEAA-provider LoTE | XML | QEAA Sign/Seal Certificates |
| EAA Providers | EAA Provider LoTE | JSON | EAA signatures |

All LoTEs use the TS 119 602 data model. TS 119 615 is used as the procedural basis for validating the LoTE structure, integrity, freshness, and trust chain. The JSON and XML signature or seal mechanisms differ; this RFC requires the applicable format-specific signature or seal verification but does not duplicate its detailed mechanics.

### 5.2 Validation Flow

The common sequence for WRP trust evaluation is:

1. Apply the artifact-specific signature and content checks.
2. Select the LoTE for the entity type.
3. Retrieve and validate the LoTE.
4. Extract the entity's Trust Anchor from `ServiceDigitalIdentity`.
5. Apply the common X.509 path validation to the presented certificate chain.
6. Apply the revocation checks defined by RFC004, wherever required.

Steps 5. and 6. SHALL be executed whenever validation of the specific Trust Artifact requires validation of an X.509 trust chain or a status check.

Successful completion of an intermediate step permits only continuation to the remaining applicable checks. A Wallet Unit or WRP SHALL NOT output a final `AUTHENTICATED`, `AUTHORIZED`, or `ACCEPTED` result until all applicable trust, path-validation, and status checks have succeeded.

- To Authenticate a WRP,
  
  - Step 1. is applied to the Credential Issuer Metadata, or Presentation Request, depending on the flow using the public key within the WRPAC transmitted within the artifact.
  - Steps 2., 3., 4. are applied to the WRPAC Provider LoTE.
  - Steps 5., 6. are applied to the WRPAC and its X.509 chain.

- To Authorize a WRP,

  - Step 1. is applied to the mandatory WRPRC transmitted in the Credential Issuer Metadata or Presentation Request. When optional Register-response validation is invoked, the applicable syntax, signature, pertinence, and consistency checks are also applied to the Register response. Step 1. is divided into (i) syntax and signature validation, (ii) authorization checks comprising WRPAC binding, issuance or presentation binding, entitlement verification, and EDP validation, and (iii) the applicable user-override behavior. These checks are described in the [Authorization Process Profile](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#evaluation-procedures).
  - Steps 2., 3., 4. are applied to the WRPRC Provider LoTE and, when optional Register-response validation is invoked, to the Registrar LoTE.
  - Step 5. is applied to the WRPRC signing certificate and its X.509 chain and, when optional Register-response validation is invoked, to the Register-response signing certificate and its X.509 chain.
  - Step 6. is applied to both the WRPRC and (if needed) the WRPRC signing certificate and its X.509 chain. When optional Register-response validation is invoked, Step 6. is also applied to the Register-response signing certificate and its X.509 chain wherever required by RFC004.

- To Validate the Signature of a WIA, KA, PID, QEAA, EAA, PuB-EAA,

  - Step 1. is applied to the Attestation using the public key within the Sign/Seal Certificate transmitted within the Attestation itself.
  - Steps 2., 3., 4. are applied to the specific Entity LoTE.
  - Steps 5., 6. are applied to the Sign/Seal Certificate and its X.509 chain.
  - Step 6. is applied to both the Attestation and the Sign/Seal certificate and its X.509 chain.

## 6. Normative Requirements

Unless otherwise stated, these requirements apply to trust evaluation interactions.

### 6.1 Wallet Unit Requirements

A Wallet Unit or WRP conforming to this RFC SHALL:

- support validation of JSON and XML LoTEs according to the applicable entity-type profile as specified in [Trust Anchor Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#trust-anchor-validation-process);
- select the LoTE according to the type of entity or Attestation being validated;
- retrieve the Trust Anchor from the validated LoTE `ServiceDigitalIdentity`;
- implement the common X.509 path validation in the X.509 Certificate Chain Validation process in the [Trust Evaluation Process](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/);
- perform WRPAC validation as specified in [Authentication Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#authentication-process);
- validate a WRPRC as specified in [Authorization Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#authorization-process);
- perform Sign/Seal Certificate and Attestation signature validation as specified in the [Trust Evaluation Process](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/);

A Wallet Unit or WRP MAY support optional Register-response validation. When it invokes this validation, it SHALL validate the response as specified in [Register Response Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#register-validation-procedure).

For this profile, references to Authorization Validation do not make Register-response validation or Register fallback mandatory. A missing or invalid mandatory WRPRC SHALL fail authorization.

### 6.2 WP2 Operated Entities

A Provider of WRPAC, WRPRC, or Sign/Seal Certificates SHALL:

- be represented in the dedicated entity-type LoTE;
- publish the Trust Anchor certificate in its `ServiceDigitalIdentity`;
- ensure that certificate identity data is consistent with the entity identity in the LoTE;
- expose the endpoint or service required by the applicable artifact profile, including revocation checks.

The LoTE Publisher SHALL:

- manage and publish one LoTE per entity type used by the pilot;
- publish each LoTE in its declared JSON or XML format;
- maintain the integrity, authenticity, and freshness information required by the applicable LoTE profile;
- publish the QEAA-provider/QTSP Trust Anchors in the dedicated QEAA-provider LoTE;
- make the LoTE endpoint and format discoverable to relying implementations.

A Registrar SHALL:

- be represented in the dedicated Registrar and Register LoTE;
- publish or expose the Register API according to the [Register API OAS](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/api/register-api/);
- return signed or sealed responses that can be verified with a Trust Anchor in the Registrar and Register LoTE.

## 7. Interface Definitions

These interfaces define the minimum observable behavior for conformance testing.

### 7.1 LoTE Endpoint

- **Direction:** Wallet Unit or WRP to the LoTE Publisher.
- **Method:** `GET`

The Wallet unit SHALL retrieve the LoTE for the entity type being validated.

The endpoint URI and media type SHALL be the ones published by the WP2 lead organization for that entity type. A request SHALL include:

- `Host`, containing the endpoint hostname;
- `Accept: application/json` for a JSON LoTE, or `Accept: application/xml` for an XML LoTE.

A successful response SHALL:

- return `200 OK`;
- set `Content-Type` to the corresponding JSON or XML media type;
- contain a structurally valid TS 119 602 LoTE;
- contain the signature or seal required by the published format profile.

The Wallet Unit SHALL perform the LoTE validation required by Section 6.1 and [Trust Anchor Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#trust-anchor-validation-process) before using any Trust Anchor from the response.

### 7.2 Register Endpoint

- **Direction:** Wallet Unit to Registrar/Register API.
- **Method:** `GET`
- **Authentication:** Public query access, unless the applicable API profile requires otherwise.

The endpoint SHALL be implemented according to the [Register API OAS](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/api/register-api/).

Use of this endpoint is OPTIONAL for Wallet Unit and WRP conformance. When a Wallet Unit invokes optional Register-response validation, it SHALL construct a query containing the WRP identifier and the context-specific intended-use or Attestation information. The response SHALL:

- return the status defined by the API for the query result;
- return a signed or sealed response for a successful query;
- use the media type specified by the OAS;
- be validated according to Section 6.1 and [Register Response Validation](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/#register-validation-procedure).

An HTTP success status alone SHALL NOT make a Register response trusted.

If the response is unavailable or fails authenticity, integrity, or pertinence validation, the Wallet Unit SHALL discard it and continue authorization using the valid mandatory WRPRC. If a response passes those checks but is inconsistent with the mandatory WRPRC, the Wallet Unit SHALL fail authorization and SHALL NOT offer a user override.

---

## 8. Security Considerations

Implementations SHALL use HTTPS for LoTE and Register retrieval and MAY apply caching and endpoint privacy measures consistent with the applicable pilot profile.

Trust Anchors SHALL NOT be accepted from an unvalidated LoTE or from self-declared interaction data.

Certificates and Certificate chains SHALL NOT be accepted solely because a leaf signature verifies.

---

## 9. Conformance

A Wallet Solution or WRP implementation conforms to this RFC if:

1. implements the requirements in Section 6.1;
2. implements the LoTE interface in Section 7.1 and, if it claims optional Register-response validation, the Register interface in Section 7.2;
3. passes the common, WRPAC, mandatory WRPRC, and Sign/Seal checks in Section 10, and passes the optional Register-response checks if it claims that capability.
