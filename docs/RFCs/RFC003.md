## 1. Introduction

This document defines the APTITUDE Trust Evaluation Profile for interoperable trust evaluation across the APTITUDE pilots. It profiles three processes from the [APTITUDE Trust Specifications](https://aptitude-consortium.github.io/wp2-trust-specifications/latest/sections/trust-evaluation-process/):

- [Authentication][wp2-authn] establishes the identity of the interacting Wallet-Relying Party.
- [Authorization][wp2-authz] determines whether an issuance or presentation operation is permitted.
- [Sign/Seal Validation][wp2-signseal] establishes the authenticity and integrity of an Attestation.

---

## 2. Scope

This RFC covers trust evaluation during issuance and presentation. It applies to the validating Wallet Unit or WRP and to the supporting certificate, trust-source, and registration components defined in [Section 4](#4-roles-and-components).

It does not define sector-specific artifact semantics, entity lifecycle administration, or revocation endpoint protocols. Revocation processing is defined by [RFC004](RFC004.md).

This RFC is a cross-pilot baseline. A pilot MAY add stricter requirements, but it SHALL NOT weaken the mandatory checks defined here.

## 4. Roles and Components

### 4.1 Wallet Unit

The Wallet Unit performs trust evaluation on behalf of the User. Its Wallet Instance performs the authorization behavior assigned to it by the Authorization Process.

### 4.2 Wallet-Relying Party (WRP)

The Wallet-Relying Party (WRP) is the entity authenticated during an interaction. Depending on the interaction, it may be a direct Relying Party or a Relying Party Intermediary.

### 4.3 Provider of WRPAC

The Provider of WRPAC issues Wallet-Relying Party Access Certificates.

### 4.4 Provider of WRPRC

The Provider of WRPRC issues Wallet-Relying Party Registration Certificates.

### 4.5 Attestation Provider

An Attestation Provider issues Attestations and signs or seals them using a Sign/Seal Certificate. The certificate issuer, Attestation Provider, and trust-source signing authority are distinct identities.

### 4.6 LoTE Provider

The LoTE Provider operates and distributes LoTEs. It is distinct from the Official Journal of APTITUDE (OJA) publication, which supplies pilot discovery information.

### 4.7 Registrar

The Registrar operates the Register and exposes its API. It is distinct from the LoTE Provider.

### 4.8 Trusted List Provider

WP2 acts as the Trusted List Provider for the pilot QEAA TL and maintains its publication.

### 4.9 List of Trusted Lists Scheme Operator (LOTLSO)

WP2 acts as the LOTLSO for the pilot LOTL and maintains TL discovery and list-signing authorization information.

## 6. Normative Requirements

An implementation SHALL satisfy the applicable validation conditions and expected behavior in [Section 9](#9-conformance-check-catalogue) for its role, operation, and artifact profile. The procedures below define their responsibilities and boundaries; their detailed checks and parameters are specified only in the catalogue.

### 6.1 Reusable Validation Procedures

#### 6.1.1 Artifact Parsing and Integrity

The validating entity SHALL establish the profile conformance and cryptographic integrity of each signed artifact using the applicable process-specific checks and the common [Signature validation catalogue](#946-signature-validation).

#### 6.1.2 LoTE Validation

The validating entity SHALL authenticate the applicable LoTE and obtain the eligible Trust Anchors using the [LoTE validation catalogue](#941-lote-validation).

#### 6.1.3 X.509 Certificate Chain Validation

The validating entity SHALL establish certificate-path trust using the [X.509 validation catalogue](#944-x509-certificate-path-validation), invoking [RFC004](RFC004.md#1013-common-conformance-checks) for the applicable revocation processing.

#### 6.1.4 TL and LOTL Validation

The validating entity SHALL authenticate the list hierarchy and establish the service and qualification information needed for the QEAA branch using the [LOTL](#942-lotl-validation) and [TL validation catalogues](#943-tl-validation).

### 6.2 Authentication Process Requirements

The Wallet Unit SHALL establish the interacting WRP's identity through the sub-processes mapped in [Section 5.2](#52-trust-evaluation-sub-processes), applying the [Authentication catalogue](#91-authentication-process).

### 6.3 Authorization Process Requirements

The Wallet Instance SHALL determine whether the operation is permitted using the authenticated interaction context, authoritative registration data, and applicable disclosure policy, applying the [Authorization catalogue](#92-authorization-process) and common [Authorization validation checks](#945-authorization-validation).

### 6.4 Sign/Seal Validation Process Requirements

The validating Wallet Unit or WRP SHALL establish Attestation authenticity and issuer trust, including qualification for the QEAA branch, by applying the [Sign/Seal catalogue](#93-signseal-validation-process) and its applicable common checks.

### 6.5 Certificate Providers and Register Requirements

The Providers of WRPAC, WRPRC, and Sign/Seal Certificates SHALL perform certificate issuance and lifecycle management according to the applicable certificate profile and [RFC004](RFC004.md). They SHALL publish the trust material required by the applicable catalogue checks. The Registrar SHALL supply the registration data needed by the [Register fallback checks](#923-optional-register-fallback).

### 6.6 Interfaces

The retrieval interfaces supply the artifacts required by validation. Their concrete requests, locations, formats, and response conditions are defined by the referenced catalogue entries.

#### 6.6.1 LoTE Retrieval

The validating entity retrieves the applicable LoTE using the discovery and retrieval checks in the [LoTE catalogue](#941-lote-validation).

#### 6.6.2 Register Retrieval

The Wallet Unit obtains registration evidence through the interface covered by the [Register fallback catalogue](#923-optional-register-fallback).

#### 6.6.3 TL and LOTL Retrieval

The validating entity obtains the list hierarchy through the discovery and retrieval checks in the [LOTL](#942-lotl-validation) and [TL catalogues](#943-tl-validation).

## 7. Security Considerations

Trust evaluation relies on authenticated trust sources, validated artifacts, and authorization data bound to the interaction. The check catalogue includes the corresponding rejection and acceptance conditions. Revocation evidence validation is covered by [RFC004](RFC004.md#1013-common-conformance-checks).

## 9. Conformance Check Catalogue

This catalogue contains the concrete validation conditions, parameters, and expected outcomes used by the testbed. Process and sub-process relationships are defined in [Section 5.2](#52-trust-evaluation-sub-processes). Each row assesses one condition; where a row applies to several inputs, the testbed SHALL instantiate it separately for each applicable input. Check-ID numeric suffixes form one strictly increasing sequence across the catalogue.

For each applicable check, the testbed SHALL exercise a conforming input and, where a failure condition exists, an input that violates that condition while the other prerequisites remain satisfied. Negative-input conformance requires the specified rejection, diagnostic, or blocking behavior. Boundary cases SHALL cover applicable time, sequence, index, and path-length comparisons. Checks of OPTIONAL functionality apply when that functionality is supported or selected. A SHOULD retains its recommendation level.

An entry's References column identifies its source. “RFC profile” marks a profile constraint established by this RFC on the cited source procedure. Common checks apply to each artifact supplied by the invoking process. The actor under test is the Wallet Unit for Authentication, the Wallet Instance for Authorization, and the validating Wallet Unit or WRP for Sign/Seal Validation, unless an entry names another actor.
